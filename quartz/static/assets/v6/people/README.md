# V6 assets — people

Portraits of real, identifiable people associated with Civic Orderism, used as
identity elements on institutional pages.

## Belongs here

Genuine photographs of a named person, published with that person's approval and
referenced from the page that identifies them. The initiator portrait is used on
`/about/initiator`.

## Not here

AI-generated or AI-edited faces, stock portraits, images of people who have not
approved publication, editorial covers (`../editorial/`), organisation material
(`../organization/`), hero visuals (`../hero/`).

## Ratio / size — canonical `2 : 3` portrait

|     | Size        |
| --- | ----------- |
| Web | 1024 × 1536 |

Never upscale beyond the supplied original. A portrait is an identity element,
not a hero visual: it must stay visually subordinate to the page's text, and it
must never be used as a full-bleed background or a circular avatar.

`alt` must be a plain factual identification of the person. Do not add honorifics
or political descriptions.
